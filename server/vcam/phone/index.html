Connection drops on iPhone?

Safari can load this page past a certificate warning but may still refuse its live connection, so the camera falls back to slower requests. To fix that once:

  1. Download the local certificate and allow the profile.
  2. Settings › General › VPN & Device Management: install it.
  3. Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings: turn on full trust for "God's Eye View Local VCam CA".

The certificate only lets your phone trust this computer.
